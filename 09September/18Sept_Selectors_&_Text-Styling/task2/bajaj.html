About us

Bajaj Automobiles is one of India’s well-known automobile manufacturers, with a strong presence in the two-wheeler and three-wheeler segments. The company is part of the Bajaj Group and has built a strong reputation through its focus on innovation, engineering, quality, and customer satisfaction. Its product portfolio includes motorcycles, scooters, and three-wheelers that serve both personal and commercial transportation needs.

Over the years, Bajaj has expanded its presence beyond India and established itself as a significant player in international markets. The company continues to focus on developing modern, fuel-efficient, and technologically advanced vehicles while adapting to changing customer needsz and the growing demand for sustainable mobility.

Bajaj Automobiles represents the growth of the Indian automotive industry and demonstrates how an Indian company can build a strong domestic and international presence through innovation, manufacturing capabilities, and continuous improvement.

Who we Are? and What we do ?

History

Bajaj has a long history in the Indian automotive industry. The Bajaj Group was founded in 1926, and the company entered the automobile business by manufacturing and selling vehicles in India. Over the years, Bajaj developed a strong presence in the two-wheeler and three-wheeler markets.

The company became widely recognized for motorcycles that combined performance, reliability, and affordability. Bajaj also expanded its international operations and established partnerships to strengthen its technology and global reach.

Today, Bajaj continues to develop innovative vehicles and invest in new technologies to meet the changing needs of customers around the world.

Our goal is to provide innovative, reliable, and efficient mobility solutions for customers in India and across the world. Bajaj focuses on combining advanced technology with practical design to create vehicles that deliver performance, safety, comfort, and value.

The company also aims to contribute to sustainable transportation by developing cleaner and more efficient mobility solutions while continuously improving its products and manufacturing processes.
