Who We Are and What We Do

Hero MotoCorp is one of the world's leading manufacturers of motorcycles and scooters. The company has a strong presence in India and serves customers across several international markets.

Hero focuses on designing and manufacturing reliable, efficient, and technologically advanced two-wheelers for everyday transportation. Its product range includes motorcycles and scooters designed for different customer needs and riding preferences.

Hero MotoCorp has a long history in India's two-wheeler industry. The company was established in 1984 as a joint venture between the Hero Group of India and Honda of Japan.

Over the years, Hero became widely recognized for producing dependable and fuel-efficient motorcycles. Its products played an important role in making two-wheelers accessible to millions of customers across India.

After the partnership with Honda ended in 2010, the company adopted the name Hero MotoCorp and continued developing its own products, technologies, and global operations.

Our Goal

Our goal is to provide reliable, innovative, and accessible mobility solutions for customers around the world. Hero focuses on combining technology, performance, safety, comfort, and efficiency in its products.

The company also aims to contribute to sustainable mobility by developing cleaner and more efficient vehicles and adopting new technologies that can support the future of transportation.

Hero's approach to leadership emphasizes innovation, customer focus, integrity, teamwork, responsibility, and continuous improvement. These principles encourage employees to work collaboratively and develop solutions that create value for customers.

The company also emphasizes ethical business practices, quality, employee development, and long-term relationships with customers, partners, and communities.
