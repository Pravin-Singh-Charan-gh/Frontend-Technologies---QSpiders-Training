Who We Are and What We Do

Honda is a well-known Japanese manufacturer of motorcycles, scooters, automobiles, and other mobility products. The company has built a strong global reputation through its focus on engineering, innovation, reliability, and technology.

Honda develops motorcycles and scooters for a wide range of customers, from everyday commuters to touring enthusiasts and performance-oriented riders. Its products are known for their practical design, efficient engines, advanced technology, and dependable performance.

Honda operates in many countries and continues to develop new technologies to improve mobility, efficiency, safety, and the overall customer experience.

Honda Motor Co., Ltd. was established in Japan in 1948 by Soichiro Honda and Takeo Fujisawa. The company initially focused on producing motorized products before expanding into motorcycle manufacturing.

Honda became one of the world's major motorcycle manufacturers and expanded its operations into international markets. Its motorcycles have included commuter, sport, touring, adventure, and premium models.

Over the years, Honda continued to invest in research, motorsport, manufacturing, engine development, and new mobility technologies, helping establish the company as a major global mobility manufacturer.

Our Goal

Honda's goal is to provide reliable and enjoyable mobility products that make people's lives easier and more convenient. The company focuses on combining engineering, innovation, performance, and practical functionality.

Honda also works toward developing cleaner and more efficient mobility technologies. Through research and continuous innovation, the company aims to address environmental challenges and changing customer requirements.

Honda's philosophy emphasizes innovation, quality, respect for the individual, teamwork, and continuous improvement. The company encourages its employees to think creatively and develop technologies that can contribute to society.

Honda also places importance on customer satisfaction, responsible business practices, technological development, and creating new value through mobility. Its philosophy of pursuing dreams and challenging conventional ideas has played an important role in the company's development.
