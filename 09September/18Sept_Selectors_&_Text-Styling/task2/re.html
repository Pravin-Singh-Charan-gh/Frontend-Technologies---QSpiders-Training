Who We Are and What We Do

Royal Enfield is a well-known motorcycle manufacturer with a rich heritage and a strong global presence. The company is known for producing motorcycles that combine classic styling, modern engineering, and a distinctive riding experience.

Royal Enfield develops motorcycles for a wide range of riders, from everyday commuters and touring enthusiasts to riders who enjoy adventure and long-distance journeys. Its motorcycles are recognized for their timeless designs, characteristic engines, and strong road presence.

The company continues to expand its presence in international markets while developing new motorcycles and technologies to meet the changing needs of modern riders.

Royal Enfield has a long history that dates back to the 19th century. The first Royal Enfield motorcycle was produced in 1901, making the brand one of the oldest motorcycle names in continuous production.

The company became particularly well known for its motorcycles in India. In 1955, Royal Enfield partnered with Madras Motors to form Enfield India, which began manufacturing the iconic Bullet motorcycle in Chennai.

Over the years, Royal Enfield continued to evolve while maintaining its classic identity. Models such as the Bullet, Classic, Himalayan, Interceptor, Continental GT, and Meteor have helped strengthen the company's position in the global motorcycle market.

Our Goal

Royal Enfield's goal is to create motorcycles that encourage riders to explore, travel, and experience the joy of motorcycling. The company focuses on combining distinctive design, enjoyable performance, reliable engineering, and authentic riding experiences.

Royal Enfield also focuses on developing motorcycles that meet modern safety, emission, and technology requirements while preserving the character and identity associated with the brand.

Royal Enfield's philosophy is centered around authenticity, craftsmanship, exploration, creativity, and a strong connection between riders and their motorcycles.

The company encourages a culture of innovation and continuous improvement while respecting its heritage. Through its motorcycles, riding communities, events, and global presence, Royal Enfield aims to build a strong and lasting relationship with motorcycle enthusiasts around the world.
