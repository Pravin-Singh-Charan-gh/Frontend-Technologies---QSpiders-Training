Who We Are and What We Do

Yamaha Motor is a well-known Japanese manufacturer of motorcycles, scooters, and other mobility products. The company has built a strong global reputation through its focus on performance, engineering, innovation, and design.

Yamaha develops motorcycles and scooters for a wide range of customers, from everyday commuters to performance-oriented riders. Its products are known for combining modern technology, distinctive styling, riding performance, and practical features.

The company operates in many countries and continues to develop new technologies to meet changing customer requirements and the future needs of mobility.

Yamaha Motor was established in Japan in 1955. The company entered the motorcycle industry with the YA-1, its first motorcycle, which quickly gained recognition for its performance and engineering.

Over the years, Yamaha expanded its motorcycle portfolio and became an important manufacturer in international markets. The company developed motorcycles ranging from small commuter models to high-performance machines.

Yamaha continued to strengthen its global presence by investing in research, engineering, motorsport, manufacturing, and innovative technologies.

Our Goal

Yamaha's goal is to create exciting and reliable mobility products that provide customers with enjoyable and meaningful experiences. The company focuses on combining advanced engineering with attractive design and practical functionality.

Yamaha also works toward developing cleaner and more efficient mobility technologies. Through innovation and continuous improvement, the company aims to respond to changing environmental requirements and customer expectations.

Yamaha's leadership philosophy emphasizes innovation, creativity, customer satisfaction, quality, teamwork, and continuous improvement. The company encourages its employees to challenge conventional ideas and develop new technologies and products.

Yamaha also places importance on responsible business practices, respect for people, technological development, and creating value for customers and society.
